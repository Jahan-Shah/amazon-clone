# Project Plan: Amazon Clone

## Product direction
Rebuild the core Amazon.com shopping experience — browse, view product, cart,
checkout, order history — as a fast, clean, working e-commerce app. Built in a
24-hour timed window. Goal is a deployed, usable product judged on speed of
delivery, product judgment (what was prioritized vs. cut), and UX/UI quality —
not feature parity with the real Amazon.

## Users
- A first-time visitor who is not signed in, browsing and searching products.
- A registered shopper who creates an account, adds items to cart, and checks out.
- A returning shopper who logs back in and views past orders.
There is no seller, admin, or third-party marketplace role in this version.

## In scope (v1 features)
1. Browse homepage with category sections and a featured product grid.
2. Search with keyword query, category filter, and sort (price, rating).
3. Product detail page: image gallery, price, stock status, add-to-cart,
   star rating, customer reviews (seeded, read-only in v1).
4. Cart: add/remove/update quantity, persists across reload, subtotal shown.
5. Account: email/password signup and login via Better Auth, session-based auth.
6. Checkout: shipping address form, Stripe test-mode payment, order
   confirmation screen.
7. Order history: list of past orders and an order detail view.

## Explicitly out of scope (v1)
- Seller accounts / third-party marketplace listings.
- Product Q&A, "frequently bought together," personalized recommendations.
- Coupons, gift cards, subscribe-and-save.
- Returns/refunds workflow.
- Multiple payment methods (Stripe test card only).
- Admin/inventory management dashboard.
These are left out deliberately to protect the core flow's quality within the
time window; each is a natural v2 candidate if time remains after item 10 of
the build plan.

## Data
- **Product**: title, slug, description, price (integer cents), images,
  category, rating, review count, stock.
- **Review**: linked to product, author name, rating, body — seeded, not
  user-submitted in v1.
- **User, Session, Account, Verification**: owned and generated by Better
  Auth's Prisma adapter — not hand-written.
- **Address**: linked to Better Auth's User.id — line1, line2, city, state, zip.
- **Order**: linked to User.id and Address, status (pending/paid/shipped),
  total, timestamp.
- **OrderItem**: linked to order and product, quantity, price snapshot at
  time of purchase.
Seed data: ~80 products across 5 categories (Electronics, Home, Books,
Fashion, Toys), realistic titles/descriptions/images, 2–4 reviews each.

## Stack
- Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
- Prisma ORM + Postgres (Neon).
- Better Auth, email/password provider, Prisma adapter, session-based auth.
- Zustand (with persist middleware) for cart state.
- Stripe, test mode, via PaymentIntents + webhook.
- Deployed on Vercel.
- Money stored and computed as integer cents throughout, formatted for display only.

## Business model
Not applicable — this is a portfolio/assignment build, not a monetized product.
No real payments; Stripe runs in test mode only.

## UX decisions
- Guest browsing and search are fully open; only checkout and account pages
  require sign-in.
- Cart persists in the browser (localStorage) so it survives refresh without
  requiring login.
- Checkout is a single page (address + payment together), not a multi-step
  wizard — this is one of the deliberate "better than the original" bets,
  since Amazon's own checkout is notoriously fragmented across steps.
- Mobile-responsive throughout; this is checked explicitly in the polish pass,
  not left to chance.
- Empty states (empty cart, empty order history, no search results) are
  designed, not left blank.
